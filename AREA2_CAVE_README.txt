HOW THE CAVE SCENE IS CREATED
-----------------------------
After Unity finishes importing and compiling, the editor script automatically creates:

    Assets/_Game/Scenes/Area2_Cave.unity

If it does not appear automatically, run:

    Fallen Spires > Area 2 - Cave > Create or Rebuild Cave Scene

The generated scene is composed of normal editable Unity GameObjects. Platforms,
checkpoints, crystals, torches and moving platforms can be selected, dragged,
duplicated and resized in Scene View.

AREA 2 DESIGN
-------------
- Vertical cave level.
- Medium difficulty.
- Platforms are farther apart than Area 1.
- Jump charge begins to matter for long gaps.
- Two moving platform challenges.
- Three checkpoints.
- Optional crystals.
- Spike hazards.
- Falling below the cave respawns at the most recent checkpoint.
- Exit at the top opens the Area 2 completion panel.

HUD

You can manually move any Platform object to rebalance jump distance. For this
area, keep most vertical differences around 2.7-3.3 Unity units, with occasional
horizontal jumps around 3-4.5 units for a medium charge-jump challenge.

IMPORTANT
---------
The project was assembled without running the Unity Editor in this environment.
Unity will perform the final asset import and scene generation when you open it.
Check the Console after the first import. If the automatic scene generator is
interrupted, use the menu command above to rebuild it.
