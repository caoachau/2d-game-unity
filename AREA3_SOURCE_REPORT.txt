Gameplay structure:
- Long vertical climb from bottom to top.
- Small platforms and repeated left/right jumps.
- 3 checkpoints.
- Horizontal moving platforms.
- Spike and pendulum hazards.
- Optional gems.
- Penultimate platform shows Princess Elira dialogue.
- Dialogue does NOT pause gameplay.
- Player must charge one final horizontal jump to the princess platform.
- Touching Princess Elira completes the area and opens the completion UI.
